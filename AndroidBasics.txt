Context :
It is an object that gives your code access to information and services provided by the Android system for a particular application/component environment.

🧠 Context = your connection/handle to the Android environment.

Your Kotlin code
       ↓
    Context
       ↓
Android system
       ↓
OS services/resources

____________________________________________________________________________________________________________________

3. Why does Android need Context?

Imagine your Battery Monitor app wants battery information.

You write:

val batteryManager =
    context.getSystemService(Context.BATTERY_SERVICE)

You might wonder:

Why can't I just write getSystemService()?

Because Android needs to know which application/component is making the request.

The context provides that connection.

Conceptually:

Your BatteryScreen
       ↓
     Context
       ↓
"Give me the battery service"
       ↓
Android System
       ↓
BatteryManager
